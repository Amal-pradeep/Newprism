# SalesAgent

- Purpose: Qualify an opportunity and draft its next sales step.
- Inputs: Prospect, fit evidence, stage, legitimate contact and prior outcome.
- Outputs: Fit hypothesis, follow-up draft and next action.
- Tools: Reviewed knowledge lookup, authorized prospect read and local draft builder. Email sending and CRM mutation are disabled in Agent Home.
- Success: A founder-approved, verified lead advances to a qualified meeting.
- Owner: Amal. Status: rules-based draft, not autonomous outreach.
