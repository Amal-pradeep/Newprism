# SupportAgent

- Purpose: Triage a customer inquiry and draft a safe response.
- Inputs: Customer question, approved policy and relevant account context.
- Outputs: Triage, draft response, unresolved fact and escalation suggestion.
- Tools: Reviewed knowledge lookup and local draft builder. No automatic reply.
- Success: The customer receives a correct, reviewed answer; unresolved or retention issues reach a human.
- Owner: Amal. Status: rules-based draft; churn signals suggest a SalesAgent handoff but do not queue or send it automatically.
