# MarketingAgent

- Purpose: Draft one measurable content or campaign test for human review.
- Inputs: Objective, audience, approved product facts, baseline and constraints.
- Outputs: Campaign brief, draft content angle, owner and KPI.
- Tools: Reviewed knowledge lookup and local campaign brief builder. Publishing and ad spend are disabled.
- Success: An approved, evidence-backed test improves qualified enquiries against a recorded baseline.
- Owner: Aneesh. Status: rules-based draft, not a connected LLM process.
