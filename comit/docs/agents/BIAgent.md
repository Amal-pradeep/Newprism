# BIAgent

- Purpose: Identify a measurable change in supplied business metrics.
- Inputs: Metric values, date window, baseline, comparison and source.
- Outputs: Observation, data gaps and next investigation.
- Tools: Reviewed knowledge lookup and local calculation. No invented analytics connection.
- Success: A human verifies the signal against source data and records a decision.
- Owner: Aadil. Status: draft and review until source metrics are connected.
